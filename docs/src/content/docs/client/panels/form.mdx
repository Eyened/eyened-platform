The right-hand panel lists the **forms** of the images open in the viewer. In a grading task it is called **Grading**; when you open images from the browser (`/view`) it is called **Forms**. Each form is driven by a **FormSchema** that defines the fields and validates your input.

## Using the panel

### Rows and targets

Forms attach to a **target**: an image, an eye at a visit, a visit, an eye, or a patient. Which one depends on the form's schema (`EntityType`). A row shows the target's name; the form itself opens in the form window.

### In a grading task (Grading)

1. The task's form (`form_schema_name`) is listed first, one row per target of the open images. A row shows whether your form is **complete** or incomplete; "n of m complete" is in the section header.
2. Press **Grade** on a row with no form yet to create it and open it. Click a row with a form to open it again.
3. **Copy** on a row makes a new form with the same answers (for example to start from a colleague's grading); it joins this set. **Delete form** removes your own forms.
4. **Other forms** lists the remaining forms of these images, grouped by target.
5. The set's **comments** are at the top of Grading.
6. **Add form** at the bottom creates a form of any other schema for the selected viewport's image; it starts on the task form.

If the task names a form that does not exist, Grading says so; ask the task owner.

**Mark ready** warns when forms are not complete: a task row with no form ("not graded") or an incomplete one, and your other incomplete forms in this set. For each target only the newest of your task forms counts. **Go back** changes nothing; **Mark ready** (or **Mark ready and next**) goes on anyway.

Below the `md` breakpoint (42 rem) the set's status, save state and the combo button sit in a footer at the bottom of Grading.

### On /view (Forms)

**Add form** is at the top; below it the forms are grouped by target, under a heading per patient when images of more than one patient are open. Rows have the same Copy and Delete form actions, and a form is opened by clicking its row.

### Points (in the form window)

Activate a point field, then left-click empty image to place a point. Drag a point to move it. Del removes the point under the pointer.

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Cycle the point's grade | Click without dragging |
| Place another point on top of one | Ctrl-click on a list (does not cycle or drag) |

A **required** grade starts on the first value when the point is placed. An **optional** grade stays unset until the first click, and the form keeps a blank choice for it. Further clicks walk the values and wrap. A single landmark stays one point.

## Implementation & analysis

Clinical forms are **inserted per site**; they are not part of the default platform seed. Each `FormSchema` row defines a JSON Schema (Draft 7) and an `EntityType`. Builtin viewer schemas (ETDRS, registration) are not offered under Add form.

Configure which form a task grades with via **`TaskDefinition.TaskConfig`** (`form_schema_name`), and which sections and tools it hides via `layout.hide`. See **[Form schemas — TaskConfig](/eyened-platform/orm/form_schemas#taskconfig-for-grading-tasks)**.

- **Data model:** [Form annotations](/eyened-platform/orm/data_model/form_annotations)
- **API:** [Forms](/eyened-platform/api/forms): create, update, and list `FormAnnotation` rows
- **Validation:** `eorm validate-forms` checks schemas and existing annotation data
