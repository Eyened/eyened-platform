The **Info** panel shows metadata for the image open in the current viewer window. Use it to confirm you are grading the correct eye and visit before drawing annotations.

## Using the panel

### What it shows

- Patient, study, and series identifiers
- Device manufacturer and model
- Modality, laterality, dimensions, and acquisition fields
- Optional extra columns configured for your deployment
- The patient's other visits, newest first. Open a visit to see the external data sources configured for this panel. The visit of the open image starts expanded and is marked "this visit". The list shows at most the 200 most recent visits.

Expand the panel in the sidebar to read fields while keeping the main image in view. Visits load when the panel is expanded. No edits are made from this panel — it is read-only.
