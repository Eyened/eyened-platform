# Security policy

EyeNED processes retinal images and clinical metadata, so we treat security reports as urgent.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion. Email **eyened@erasmusmc.nl** with:

- what is affected (component, endpoint or file, and version or commit),
- how to reproduce it,
- what an attacker could do with it.

Do not include patient data or real credentials in your report. We will acknowledge your report and keep you informed while we fix it.
